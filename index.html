Our Story

Built by Industry Experts, for Gauteng Industry

Optimum Current Solutions was founded in 2022 by industry experts with over 20 years of combined experience in electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic systems. We emerged to fill a gap in the Gauteng market for a single, comprehensive industrial service provider.

Operating from our Boksburg base, we've built a reputation for excellence by delivering integrated solutions that encompass electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic disciplines, so clients deal with one accountable partner instead of juggling multiple contractors.

Our Mission

To deliver reliable, innovative, and cost-effective electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic solutions that empower businesses to operate efficiently and safely.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted partner for integrated electrical, mechanical, and pneumatic solutions, setting the standard for quality and reliability in the industry.
